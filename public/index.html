Status Checking…

Notifications

Only works while H1 is open in this browser tab — this is not a background/push notification. When on, H1 checks your homework and planner deadlines and lets you know if something is due today or overdue.

Accessibility

H1 also automatically respects your system's reduced-motion setting.

Privacy

H1 stores your conversations, notes, homework, documents, flashcards, quiz history and settings in this browser's local storage only. Nothing is uploaded except the text/image you send to the AI Tutor when you ask a question — and only the current message, not your other stored data.
